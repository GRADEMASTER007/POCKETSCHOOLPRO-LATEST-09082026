# 18 - K-12 & International Education Frameworks

## International Educational Standards Supported

### 1. Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE)
- **Levels**: Checkpoint, IGCSE, O-Level, AS & A-Level.
- **Subjects**: Extended Mathematics, Physics (0625), Chemistry (0620), Biology (0610), Economics, Computer Science (0478).

### 2. International Baccalaureate (IB)
- **Programmes**: Primary Years Programme (PYP), Middle Years Programme (MYP), Diploma Programme (DP).
- **DP Subjects**: Mathematics Analysis & Approaches (AA), Applications & Interpretation (AI), Physics HL/SL, Chemistry HL/SL.

### 3. United States Frameworks
- **Common Core State Standards (CCSS)**: K-12 Mathematics & English Language Arts.
- **Advanced Placement (AP)**: AP Calculus AB/BC, AP Physics 1/C, AP Chemistry, AP Computer Science A.
