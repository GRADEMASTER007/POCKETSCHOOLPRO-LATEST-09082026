# 08 - UI/UX Design System

## Color Palette & Luxury Aesthetics

Pocket School Pro employs a dual design atmosphere:
1. **Gold Luxury & Deep Cosmic Dark Mode**: Used on the landing page, hero section, and VIP branding.
   - Primary Gold: `#f59e0b` (Amber 500), `#d97706` (Amber 600), `#b45309` (Amber 700)
   - Cosmic Background: `#030712` (Gray 950), `#0b0f19` (Space Deep Blue)
   - Accents: Electric Indigo (`#6366f1`), Cyan Emerald (`#10b981`)
2. **Clean High-Contrast Light Mode**: Used across study tools, dashboards, and whiteboard for maximum legibility.
   - Canvas Surface: `#ffffff` (White), `#f8fafc` (Slate 50)
   - Border Accent: `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200), `#cbd5e1` (Slate 300)
   - Primary Text: `#0f172a` (Slate 900), `#1e293b` (Slate 800)
   - Secondary Text: `#64748b` (Slate 500)

---

## Typography Standards

- **Display & Headings**: Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter with tight letter-spacing (`tracking-tight`).
- **Body Text**: Inter / Sans-serif with generous line-height (`leading-relaxed`, `1.5` - `1.7`).
- **Mathematical & Code**: KaTeX LaTeX font stack and font-mono (`font-mono`, `ui-monospace`).

---

## UI Components & Micro-Interactions

- **Borders & Radius**: Clean rounded corners capped at 12px to 24px (`rounded-xl`, `rounded-2xl`, `rounded-3xl`).
- **Glassmorphism**: Subtle translucent overlays (`bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-md`, `bg-slate-900/90 backdrop-blur-lg`).
- **Animations**: Framer Motion transitions (`motion.div`), smooth hover scaling, and glowing gold focus rings.
- **Accessibility**: High contrast toggle (Yellow/Black mode), AAA color contrast compliance, and full keyboard navigation hotkeys (`Space`, `N`, `P`, `M`, `I`).
