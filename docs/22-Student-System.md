# 22 - Student System & Learning Experience

The Student System is the core engine of Pocket School Pro (`src/components/dashboards/StudentDashboard.tsx`):

1. **Daily Study Quests & XP Gamification**:
   - Students earn XP points and unlock achievements by solving math problems, completing flashcard decks, and maintaining daily study streaks.
2. **Interactive Subject Centers**:
   - Dedicated hubs for STEM (Mathematics, Physical Sciences), Humanities, Business & Accounting, and Languages.
3. **Feynman AI Whiteboard & D3 Grapher**:
   - Step-by-step problem solver with animated line-by-line LaTeX rendering and interactive coordinate canvas.
4. **Spoken AI Voice Tutor**:
   - Hands-free audio tutoring in 11 South African official languages.
