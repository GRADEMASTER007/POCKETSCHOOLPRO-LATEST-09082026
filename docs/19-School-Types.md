# 19 - School Types & Institutional Adaptations

Pocket School Pro adapts its AI persona and study recommendations based on institutional classification:

1. **Public Township & Rural Schools**:
   - Focuses on low-data bandwidth optimization, audio voice tutoring in indigenous regional languages, and step-by-step CAPS exam paper solutions.
2. **Private Independent Schools (IEB / Cambridge / IB)**:
   - Emphasizes higher-order reasoning, research synthesis, laboratory report preparation, and advanced calculus/physics.
3. **TVET Colleges & Technical Vocational Schools**:
   - Tailored for Technical Mathematics, Electrical Trade Theory, Engineering Science, Motor Mechanics, and Fitting & Turning.
4. **Homeschooling Academies & Distance Learners**:
   - Provides structured daily study roadmaps, self-paced quizzes, and automated progress reports for parents.
