# 01 - Project Overview

## Executive Summary
**Grade Master Africa – Pocket School Pro (Gold Edition)** is an enterprise 4K AI-powered academic and accessibility ecosystem designed for South Africa, the broader African continent, and international education standards.

It combines state-of-the-art multi-modal AI (Google Gemini 1.5 Pro & Gemini 1.5 Flash), Google Vision AI, KaTeX mathematical notation, D3.js interactive plotting, and web speech synthesis into an installable Progressive Web Application (PWA).

---

## Mission & Vision

### Mission
To democratize high-tier STEM, humanities, and vocational education across Africa by putting a personalized 24/7 AI Master Tutor in every student's pocket—regardless of location, economic background, or language barrier.

### Vision
To serve as the primary digital education infrastructure for K-12, TVET, higher education, and specialized academies across the continent, bridging educational inequality through local curriculum alignment, multilingual regional voice tutoring, and cloud persistence.

---

## Target Audience & User Personas

1. **K-12 Students (Grades R - 12)**:
   - Primary and Secondary school learners seeking instant step-by-step homework help, OCR problem solving, quiz generation, and exam revision.
2. **Post-Secondary & Higher Education Students**:
   - University, College, and Vocational (TVET) students needing research synthesis, academic paper search, laboratory simulations, and study roadmaps.
3. **Teachers & Educators**:
   - Lesson plan generators, rubric builders, automated grading assistants, student analytics dashboards, and curriculum management.
4. **Parents & Guardians**:
   - Real-time academic progress tracking, attendance monitoring, assignment verification, and direct AI tutor oversight.
5. **School Administrators**:
   - Bulk seat management, pooled institutional token distribution, teacher performance logs, and curriculum compliance auditing.
6. **Corporate & NGO Sponsors**:
   - "Sponsor a Learner" initiative funding individual student or school base passes through transparent ZAR payment gateways.

---

## Primary Supported Curricula & Global Coverage

- **South Africa**: CAPS (Curriculum Assessment Policy Statements) & IEB (Independent Examinations Board).
- **United Kingdom**: Cambridge Assessment International Education (IGCSE, O-Levels, A-Levels) & UK National Curriculum (EYFS, Key Stages 1-4).
- **United States**: Common Core, NGSS (Next Generation Science Standards), AP (Advanced Placement), SAT, and ACT.
- **International**: IB (International Baccalaureate - PYP, MYP, DP).
- **Regional Dialects**: isiZulu, Sesotho, Swahili, Yoruba, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sepedi, Setswana, Hausa, Igbo, Amharic, Shona, and English.
