# 17 - Curriculum System Architecture

## Supported African & International Curricula

Pocket School Pro dynamically tailors AI explanations, formulas, grading standards, and terminologies based on the user's selected curriculum.

### 1. South African CAPS (Curriculum Assessment Policy Statements)
- Grades R through 12.
- Tailored for National Senior Certificate (NSC) examinations.
- Covers Mathematics, Technical Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Accounting, Business Studies, Geography, and Agricultural Sciences.

### 2. South African IEB (Independent Examinations Board)
- Independent school curriculum standard in Southern Africa.
- Emphasizes higher-order taxonomy questions (Blooms Levels 4, 5, 6), critical analysis, and open-ended problem solving.

### 3. African Regional Curricula
- **Nigeria**: WAEC (West African Examinations Council) & NECO.
- **Kenya**: CBC (Competency-Based Curriculum) & KCSE.
- **Zimbabwe**: ZIMSEC (Zimbabwe School Examinations Council) O & A Levels.
- **Ghana**: WASSCE.
